## 2. Genre Benchmarks — Successful Action Roguelikes

| Game | What They Do | Price | Rating | Key Lesson |
|------|-------------|-------|--------|------------|
| Hades (Supergiant) | Hack-and-slash roguelike dungeon crawler, fully voiced, story events | $24.99 Steam | 98% Overwhelmingly Positive (140K+ reviews) | 1M+ copies in 10 days, 5M+ lifetime. Story + combat + replayability is the formula. |
| Dead Cells (Motion Twin) | Action platformer roguelike, tight controls, tactical combat | $24.99 Steam | 97% Overwhelmingly Positive (144K+ reviews) | 10M+ copies across all platforms. **Combat feel is everything** — players forgive rough edges if hitting feels great. |
| Balatro (LocalThunk) | Poker-themed roguelike deckbuilder (solo dev) | $14.99 Steam / $6.99 mobile | Universal acclaim. GotY 2024. | 5M copies sold. Solo dev. Budget is not an excuse. Mobile port = second revenue wave. |
| Slay the Spire (Mega Crit) | Deck-building roguelike | $24.99 Steam / $9.99 mobile | Overwhelmingly Positive | Reference for an entire subgenre. Tight mechanic beats feature bloat. |

**What makes them all win:**
- Satisfying moment-to-moment gameplay loop
- Strong replayability through build variety
- Distinctive identity (mythology, setting, art style)
- "Overwhelmingly Positive" Steam rating drives algorithmic discovery

| Ludo King (Gametion) | Digitized Indian board game | Free (IAP) | 1.25B+ downloads. Cultural relevance drove long-term success. |

**Key mobile insight:**
- India: 8.45B game installs in FY 2024-25 BUT IAP revenue only ~$400M (low ARPU)
- 70% of Indian mobile gaming businesses believe local language/culture content is crucial growth factor
- **Strategy:** Steam/international = revenue priority. Mobile = volume/awareness play.

## 4. Market Positioning — Is "Indian Police Roguelike" Viable?

**Verdict: YES. Here's why:**

- ~62% of roguelike releases on Steam failed to recoup dev costs in 2024 — but that's for *generic* roguelikes. **Strong cultural identity beats saturation.**
- No direct competitor exists globally in this space
- Indian publishers expanding beyond domestic success (US, Saudi Arabia, UK)
- "Singham/cop action" + Bollywood energy is **extremely streamable** for Twitch/YouTube
- Indian + diaspora audiences have never seen themselves in this genre

**Risk:** Must deliver on gameplay fundamentals first. Cultural exportability works, but the game must meet international quality standards.
