## 1. IAA Done Right — Mobile Action/Roguelike Games

### Archero
- **Ad formats:** Rewarded video, limited interstitials, event ads, daily reward ads
- **Where ads appear:** Revive after death, extra loot wheel, bonus energy, additional chests, end-of-run reward multipliers
- **Feels fair:** Revive optional, rewards meaningful, ads appear after action (never during combat)
- **Feels predatory:** Aggressive energy systems, too many progression walls, constant popups
- **Lesson:** Ads tied to saving a run / improving rewards / speeding progression. Never mandatory.

### Soul Knight
- **Ad formats:** Rewarded video only (mostly), minimal forced ads
- **Where ads appear:** Free character trials, extra revive, bonus gems, temporary buffs
- **Why accepted:** No combat interruptions, no ad spam, lightweight but useful rewards
- **Lesson:** Gameplay stays sacred. Players tolerate ads when quality stays high.

### PunBall
- **Ad formats:** Rewarded video, interstitials between sessions, event reward boosts
- **Where ads appear:** Continue after defeat, double rewards, daily event bonuses, equipment rolls
- **Lesson:** Heavy monetization of progression acceleration. Works because progression is grind-heavy. But too much friction can push players away.

### Survivor.io
- **Ad formats:** Rewarded video, optional revive ads, bonus chest ads
- **Where ads appear:** Mid-run death revive, daily rewards, gear chest bonuses, XP multipliers
- **Lesson:** Short sessions + frequent reward moments = many natural ad opportunities without breaking flow.

## 2. Rewarded Ad Best Practices

### Rewards Players Actually Watch For

| Reward Type | Why It Works |
|-------------|--------------|
| Revives | Saves strong runs — emotional investment |
| Double rewards | Faster progression |
| Rare loot chance | Curiosity + excitement |
| Extra rerolls | Helps optimization |
| Temporary buffs | Makes next run easier |
| Premium currency fragments | Feels valuable |

### Rewards That Fail

| Weak Reward | Why Ignored |
|-------------|-------------|
| Tiny coin amounts | Feels meaningless |
| Generic filler items | No emotional value |
| Forced "watch for energy" systems | Feels manipulative |
| Long cooldown skips | Frustrating design |

### Sweet Spot: 3–6 rewarded ads per 20–30 minute session

Enough to monetize engaged players without fatigue, irritation, or session abandonment.

### Danger Zone (Too Many Ads)
- Ad offered after EVERY floor
- Multiple popups stack together
- Revive ads appear repeatedly in same run

Players begin feeling "this game exists for ads" instead of "ads support the game."

## 3. Indian Mobile Gaming Ads

### Best Ad Networks for India

| Network | Why |
|---------|-----|
| **Google AdMob** | Largest fill rate in India, stable for rewarded ads |
| **Unity Ads** | Strong for gaming audiences and rewarded videos |
| **AppLovin** | High-performing rewarded/interstitial optimization |
| ironSource | Popular hybrid monetization solution |
| Meta Audience Network | Useful for scale but weaker for gaming-focused rewards |

### Typical Indian eCPM Estimates

| Ad Type | India eCPM |
|---------|-----------|
| Rewarded Video | ~$2–$8 |
| Interstitial | ~$0.5–$2 |
| Banner Ads | ~$0.1–$0.5 |

**Key insight:** Indian volume is huge, but revenue per player is lower than US/UK/Japan. Retention matters more. Global audience should still be targeted.

### Indian Games That Monetized Well with Ads
- **Ludo King** — Massive ad-driven business. Strong culturally familiar identity boosts retention.
- **Subway Surfers** (huge Indian audience despite not being Indian-made) — Short sessions + repeatable loops = excellent rewarded ad economy.

## 4. Best IAA Structure for Constable

### During Runs — ONLY:
- Revive ads (emotional investment in run)
- Rare chest opportunities

### After Runs:
- Reward multipliers
- Seva Point recovery
- Bonus loot

### In Hub (Police Station):
- Daily rewards
- Preparation buffs
- Reroll bonuses

### What to AVOID Completely:
- Banner ads during gameplay ❌
- Forced interstitial after every run ❌
- Energy timers ❌
- Ads before combat ❌
- Fake scarcity mechanics ❌

These destroy immersion instantly in action roguelikes.

---

## Final Strategic Insight

> **Ads should enhance momentum, not interrupt momentum.**

Players happily watch ads if they feel: **rewarded, rescued, empowered, curious.**
Players quit when ads feel: **forced, spammy, progression-blocking, disconnected from gameplay.**

That's the core difference between a sustainable ad economy and a frustrating mobile game.
