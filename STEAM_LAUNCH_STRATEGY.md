## 1. Steam Page Optimization

### Tags — Priority Mapping

| Priority | Tag | Why |
|----------|-----|-----|
| 🔴 Must (Top 5) | Action Roguelike | Core genre, highest-traffic roguelike tag on Steam |
| 🔴 Must (Top 5) | Roguelite | Broader net; captures players who hate permadeath-heavy games |
| 🔴 Must (Top 5) | Hack and Slash | Lathi combat = exactly this |
| 🔴 Must (Top 5) | Dungeon Crawler | 25-floor descending structure fits perfectly |
| 🔴 Must (Top 5) | Difficult | Signals challenge; roguelike fans actively seek this |
| 🟡 Strong | Singleplayer | Table stakes |
| 🟡 Strong | Isometric | Camera angle helps Steam's "More Like This" |
| 🟡 Strong | Story Rich | Corruption narrative |
| 🟡 Strong | Atmospheric | Basti slums → marble halls visual range |
| 🟡 Strong | Replay Value | Every roguelike needs this |
| 🟢 Secondary | Pixel Graphics | If art direction goes that way |
| 🟢 Secondary | Perma Death | Accurate but slightly narrows audience |
| ❌ Avoid as Top 5 | Indie | Community adds it anyway; wastes a top-5 slot |

**Golden rule:** Top 5 tags carry most algorithmic weight. Action Roguelike, Roguelite, Hack and Slash in top 3 = appear in Hades/Dead Cells "More Like This" sections. Free marketing.

### Short Description Formula

**Template from Balatro:**
> *"The poker roguelike. Balatro is a hypnotically satisfying deckbuilder where you play illegal poker hands, discover game-changing jokers, and trigger satisfying combos."*

**Apply to Constable:**
> *"The Indian cop roguelike. Fight corruption floor by floor as Beat Constable Arjun Rathore — from chaotic Basti slums to the Commissioner's marble halls — in this action roguelike where every run exposes a new layer of rot."*

Hook → Genre → Unique setting.

### Screenshot Order

| # | What to Show | Why |
|---|-------------|-----|
| 1 | Best combat moment — lathi mid-combo, enemies flying, HUD visible | First impression; must scream "this feels good to play" |
| 2 | Dungeon/floor layout — BSP rooms, procedural corridors | Shows roguelike structure immediately |
| 3 | Weapon upgrade screen or Warrant card | Shows progression depth, build variety |
| 4 | Environmental contrast — Basti slums vs. a grander floor | Sells visual journey and scope |
| 5–8 | Enemy encounters, boss room, civilian rescue, Hub/station | Breadth of content |

Steam allows 20 screenshots but most users only look at first 5-7. Valve is explicit: screenshots must show actual gameplay — no concept art, pre-renders, or marketing copy.

### Capsule Art

- 0-3 words maximum on the capsule image
- Must immediately communicate genre + what players will be doing
- For Constable: Arjun in confident cop pose, lathi raised, corrupt silhouette behind him, game title in bold
- Background hinting at slum-to-marble journey

## 2. Early Access Best Practices

### Timing

| Aspect | Recommendation |
|--------|---------------|
| Duration | 6-12 months in Early Access. Hades did 2 years. Dead Cells did 1 year. Balatro skipped EA entirely. |
| Content threshold | Minimum: 15/25 floors, 3/5 bosses, 12/20 Warrants, core meta progression. Players pay for a taste, not a tech demo. |
| Launch price | $7.99 EA → $9.99 at 1.0. Proven Hades strategy. |
| Updates | Bi-weekly or monthly content drops. Dead Cells maintained momentum with regular updates through EA. |
| Community | Active dev blog + Discord during EA. Hades built its cult following through dev transparency. |

### What Works in EA for Roguelikes

| Strategy | Example | Result |
|----------|---------|--------|
| Regular content patches | Dead Cells | Sustained interest for 1+ year EA |
| Dev transparency / blogs | Hades | Built community before 1.0 |
| Player feedback integration | Slay the Spire | Core mechanics refined by EA community |
| Demo during Steam Next Fest | Hades, Dead Cells | Massive wishlist spikes |

### What Fails

| Pitfall | Why |
|---------|-----|
| Too little content at EA launch | Negative reviews stick even after content is added |
| No roadmap | Players lose trust |
| Too long in EA (2+ years with no updates) | Community dies and never comes back |
| Silent development | No communication = assumed dead project |

**Recommendation:** Aim for 6-8 month EA with monthly content drops. Launch EA during or right after a Steam Next Fest.

### Press Hook

**"The First Indian Police Roguelike"** is the headline. Additional angles:

1. **Cultural representation** — "Hades meets Singham" is instantly understandable
2. **Underdog story** — Small team, Indian dev, budget game against AAA
3. **Bollywood energy** — Corrupt cops, dramatic lathi combat, Hindi dialogue
4. **Steam page launch** — Pitch "Help us decide the direction" community involvement

### Steam Next Fest

- Run a 30-60 minute demo with first 3 floors and 1 boss
- Engage streamers during Next Fest week
- Demo should end with a "Wishlist now" prompt
- Hades and Dead Cells both credit Next Fest demos as launchpad moments
